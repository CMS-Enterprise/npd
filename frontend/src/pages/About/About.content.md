# What is the National Provider Directory?

The National Provider Directory (NPD) beta is a first step towards building a comprehensive database of all healthcare providers in the United States. The directory combines data from multiple CMS data systems to create a unified dataset of healthcare providers.

This tool helps you find qualified healthcare professionals, verify their credentials, and determine their Medicare participation status.

Our directory includes information about:

- Healthcare providers and their specialties
- Accurate provider and organizational hierarchies
- Practice and facility addresses
- Licensing and credentialing information
- Medicare provider participation status
- NPI (National Provider Identification) numbers
- Interoperability details at each location, including FHIR endpoints

# How to search for providers

**Step 1: Start your search**
Visit the search page and enter your search criteria. You can search by provider name, organization, or NPI.

**Step 2: Review provider information**
Browse the search results to find providers that meet your needs. Tags at the top of the profile will show you which providers are IAL-2 verified, participate in Medicare, an Click "View profile" to see detailed information.

**Step 3: Verify information**
Before scheduling an appointment, always contact the provider's office directly to verify information, check insurance acceptance, and confirm they are accepting new patients.

# Long-term vision: Helping patients find the care they need

The long-term goal of the National Provider Directory is to improve access to healthcare by creating a single, trusted resource for provider and payer information across the U.S. healthcare system. CMS is working to address the problems caused by today's fragmented, inconsistent, and burdensome provider directories, which make it harder for patients to find quality care and for providers to keep their information accurate and up to date.

# Long-term vision: Connecting a fragmented system

An accurate, connected directory is essential infrastructure for modern healthcare. As part of the [Health Technology Ecosystem](https://www.cms.gov/) initiative, the National Provider Directory supports bold modernization efforts—consolidating data, removing strain on patients, providers, and the national budget, and strengthening care coordination nationwide through interoperability and seamless data exchange.

The National Provider Directory will serve as the digital map of U.S. healthcare, linking the people, organizations, and systems that deliver care every day. This includes health plan network data—giving patients a clear view of which providers are in-network with their insurance plan, eliminating guesswork, and reducing unexpected costs.

The directory will also support a reimagined provider registration and enrollment process, giving providers a single place to apply for an NPI and manage their Medicare enrollments.

# Building the foundation together

The directory is being developed as a shared public resource, maintained collectively by CMS, providers, health plans, and other partners to ensure data stays accurate and up to date. In support of transparency and public access, the NPD is an open source project. Explore the code and contribute via our developer documentation([link open source section on dev documentation](/developers#open-source-project)).

# Accounts and secure authentication options

Providers can create accounts on the directory to claim and maintain their records. In time, the directory will unify the separate login experiences of NPPES and PECOS to include all NPI registration and PECOS payment information.

To review or update your provider record, you'll need to authenticate using one of our trusted identity verification services. If you already have an account with any of these services, you can use it here.
